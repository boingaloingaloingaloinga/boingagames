Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

The short version: accounts need no email. We store your username, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, your profile, your friends and your messages so the site works. We don't sell your data. The site also uses Google Analytics and Google AdSense, which use cookies. You can delete your account and its data at any time.

1. What we collect

If you make an account, our server stores:

  • Username and password. Your password is stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash; we never store or see it in plain text.
  • Profile: bio, username colour, profile picture, banner, and when you joined or last changed your username.
  • Friends: your friends, friend requests and blocked users.
  • Messages: direct messages and the images/GIFs you send.
  • Status: whether you're active or away, the game you're playing on the site, and the last off-site game you opened, along with when we last heard from your browser.
  • Activity: your most played games and playtime (from the site's statistics), if "Show my activity" is on.
  • Reports you send about other users.
  • Login sessions: a random login token (stored hashed) so you stay logged in, for up to 60 days of inactivity.
  • IP address (short-term): used only to limit sign-up and log-in attempts and stop abuse. These records are automatically deleted after they expire (within about a day).

We do not ask for your email, real name, birthday, phone number or location.

Stored in your browser only (not sent to us unless noted above): your settings, theme, favorites, statistics, and your login token.

2. Who can see what

  • Logged-in users can see your username, picture, banner, bio, join date and status.
  • Your current game and "just went to" game are shown unless you turn off Show what I'm playing.
  • Your most played games are shown unless you turn off Show my activity.
  • Always appear offline hides your online status from everyone.
  • Messages are only shown to you and the person you're talking to. They are not end-to-end encrypted; the site administrator may review reported messages or look at data when needed to stop abuse or follow the law.
  • People you block can't see your profile, find you in search, friend you or message you.

3. Services we use

  • Cloudflare hosts the account server, database and uploaded images (Cloudflare privacy policy).
  • Google Analytics measures how people use the site using cookies.
  • Google AdSense shows ads. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalised ads at Google Ad Settings. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Games that open on other websites have their own privacy policies.

We don't sell your personal information or share it with anyone else, except if required by law or to protect people's safety.

4. How long we keep it

  • Chat images and GIFs: deleted automatically after about 30 days.
  • Old profile pictures and banners: deleted when you replace or remove them.
  • Messages: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Everything else: until you delete your account.

5. Deleting your data

Go to Edit profile → Danger zone → Delete my account. This deletes your account, profile, pictures, friends, blocks, messages you sent and images you sent. Messages other people sent you are deleted when they delete them or their accounts.

6. Children

Accounts are only for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed, login tokens are stored hashed, and all connections use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top.
